Lasagna

Description

Classic lasagna with ground beef and whole wheat noodles

Ingredients

  • 12 whole wheat lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano, or to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 package cottage cheese
  • 1/2 cup shredded Parmesan cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 1/2 cups tomato-basil pasta sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat

    2. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F

  2. Cook the noodles

    3. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add lasagna noodles and cook for 10 minutes or until al dente; drain.

  3. Cook the beef

    4. Meanwhile, place ground beef, garlic, oregano, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper in a large skillet over medium heat; cook and stir until beef is crumbly and evenly browned, about 10 minutes.

  4. Mix the cheese with eggs

    5. Mix cottage cheese, Parmesan cheese, and eggs together in a large bowl until thoroughly combined.

  5. Lay the noodles

    6. Lay 4 noodles side by side on the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking pan; top with a layer of prepared tomato-basil sauce, a layer of ground beef mixture, and a layer of cottage cheese mixture. Repeat layers twice more, ending with a layer of sauce; sprinke mozzarella cheese on top. Cover the dish with aluminium foil.

  6. Bake

    7. Bake in the preheated oven until the lasagna is bubbling and the cheese has melted, about 30 minutes. Remove foil and bake until cheese has begun to brown, about 10 more minutes. Allow to stand at least 10 minutes before serving.
