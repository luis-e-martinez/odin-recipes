Cookies

Description

Make your own cookies with crips edges, chewy centers and melty chocolate chips!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 teaspoons hot water
  • 1 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup chopped walnuts

Steps

  1. Preheat and mix

    2. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F. Beat butter, white sugar, and brown sugar together in a large bowl with an electric mixer until smooth and creamy.

  2. Add eggs and vanilla

    3. Beat in eggs, one at a time, then stir in vanilla.

  3. Disolve the baking soda

    4. Dissolve baking soda in hot water; add to batter along with salt and mix until combined.

  4. Add the flour, chicolate chips and walnuts

    5. Stir in flour, chocolate chips, and walnuts until a soft dough forms.

  5. Divide the dough

    6. Drop rounded spoonfuls of cookie dough 2 inches apart onto unreased baking sheets

  6. Bake

    7. Bake in the preheated oven until edges are lightly browned, about 10 minutes.

  7. Let it cool

    8. Cool on the baking sheets briefly before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.

  8. Enjoy!

    9. Serve and enjoy! Or if you preffer, store them.
